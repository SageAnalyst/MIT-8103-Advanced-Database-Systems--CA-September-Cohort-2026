Case Study


The main users of the database are:
* Inventory & warehouse managers
* Sales & finance analysts
* Digital marketing / media strategists
* Logistics & dispatch coordinators


Major Data to be Stored
The database will store information relating to:
* Product catalog details (product names, colors, sizes, and launch dates)
* Sales transactions (order dates, quantities sold, and unit prices)
* Customer fulfillment locations (delivery street addresses and destination states)
* Logistics delivery fees
* Marketing outreach channels (WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, and Website)




Key Database Operations


The database will support the following operations:


* Add and update product catalog records
* Query available apparel by size, color, and launch date
* Record sales transactions and batch orders
* Log fulfillment addresses and state-level delivery destinations
* Track delivery fees and determine regional shipping costs
* Attribute customer orders to marketing channels
* Compute outreach conversion rates and order volumes per channel
* Calculate total and average revenue per product collection
* Evaluate longitudinal sales performance across fiscal periods
* Generate summary reports on product popularity by color and size




Purpose of the Database


The purpose of the Product_mod database is to provide a structured and reliable system for managing apparel inventory, tracking retail sales performance, attributing customer acquisition to promotional channels, and overseeing regional fulfillment logistics for Xoxho Xpress.
